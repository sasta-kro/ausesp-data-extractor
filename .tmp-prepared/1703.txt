ABSTRACT

Today, the restaurant business has been growing rapidly. Every
restaurant tries to present something new and unique to its customers.
Many successful restaurants attract customers, but many of them fail. A
contributing factor in addition to food, the more important is the
service that is offered. The system integrates restaurant management, or
simply called restaurant management system, will greatly helping
restaurants to provide faster and better service to its customers, at
the same time providing reliable control for the management of the
restaurant. that's why we want to try to apply an online-based system to
facilitate transactions and provide convenience for customers.
Restaurant management system can handle the overall operations of the
restaurant, from the moment customers arrive, order a meal, food
preparation, up until customers make a payment and left the restaurant.

CHAPTER 1

INTRODUCTION

Nowadays, many restaurants are trying to provide a better quality
service to the customers to compete with other restaurants; so does the
AMALIA Restaurant. Amalia restaurant is located in Timor-Leste/Baucau
Villa, in the East of Timor-Leste. This restaurant was established since
1980s, the staffs of this restaurant around 20, two persons as chef, 1
as manager, and the rest as usual staffs or waiters and waitress. The
dishes or menu that provided by restaurant are: European foods, Asian
foods especially Indonesia foods, Australian foods and Timorese foods.
This restaurant is quite attracting to many foreigners, as a day there
are about 500 people visit or having meal in this restaurant. So, we
think that would be good if they apply online-based system to ease the
transaction and giving convenience to the customers who visit or having
meal in this restaurant.

1.1. EXISTING SYSTEM

The existing system in this restaurant is still in manual system which
is customers come to the restaurant and find for the available seat,
after that the waiters bring the menu to them and customers looking
through the given menu for ordering food. After they get the menu they
want then they list them down on the piece of paper which is provided by
the waiters/waitress and give the paper to the waiters and the waiters
bring that to the kitchen and give to the chef for preparing or cooking
the ordering dishes. After foods are finished cooking, then the chef
will ring the bell signed that the food has been ready to serve so
waiters pick the foods from kitchen and serve to customers. After
customers finished eating then they will call the waiters for the bill
and the payment will be done by cash, and here waiter must use the
calculator for calculating the transaction and the overall process is
done.

1.2. STATEMENT OF PROBLEMS

Since the Amalia restaurant is in manual-based system, it makes
customers are uncomfortable in ordering meals as well as hold up/making
the transaction process delay and it is affecting to low income or
profit.

Following are the problems that customers, staffs and manager are
facing:

a)  Ordering meal written in paper

Since every ordering food are written in a piece of paper, so it’s hard
for the chef or even staff to read the customers’ handwriting.

b)  Menu picture/book is not Clear and flexible

Since the Menu pictures are in the piece of paper (A4 paper) so it does
not look good for the customer as it’s sometimes hard to glance at
because the picture is too small to be seen. As well as the Menu book is
too big and sometimes customers have to go back and forth through each
page to find the dish.

c)  Manual Calculation is applied

Once customers finish eating and making the payment, the process of
calculation is still in calculator-based, so miscalculation or error can
be rise or occurred.

d)  No Bill and report

Since there are no Bill and report, they always count the daily income
at the end of the day such as at night counting how much profit they
earn for each day.

e)  Ordering process is longer

As staff has to go back and forth taking the customer order to kitchen
which takes so much time.

1.3. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

The main objective of this project is to ease the existing restaurant
management system to give the best quality service for its customers.

1.  To design a user-friendly digital menu for both customer and staff
    to interact with.

- Customer: a digital menu on screen will be provided on the table hence
  the customer can make selection of various categories of food.

- Staff: a computer for the waiter to take order for ‘Take away’, check
  status of food and checkout.

- Chef: a list of orders will be shown on screen where the chef can
  manage the orders. After the order has been selected and updated its
  status; such as: Waiting, Preparing (for Drink only), Cooking, Ready
  to be served and Done.

- Admin: can add/delete/update menu and table management as well as
  check and print reports for owner.

1.4. LIMITATION

- There are several things that can’t be done by our system which is
  inventory management system as well as the system does not accept
  Card-Payment method, the payment must be done by Cash only.

CHAPTER 2

SYSTEM ANALYSIS

There are 4types of users in our system which are: Admin, Customer,
Staff and Chef. The requirement or role for each user are presented as
follow:

Admin role

- To create, edit, update tables.

- To add, edit, delete menus.

- To manage food categories; add, edit, delete categories.

- To check daily, monthly, yearly reports for manager.

- Check which item is sold most in each day and month

Staff role

- Authorize table for customers when they are dining in.

- Take order directly from customers ordering for 'Take away'.

- Get food status notification from kitchen. Example; waiter can take
  the food when the notification says 'Ready to be served'.

- Checkout and print bill.

Customer role

- Browse food menu from digital touch-screen tablet.

- Make order directly from the tablet.

- Check order list

- Check food status

- Check bill

Chef role

- Receive food order from customer table

- Update food status

  2.1. Work Flow

[]

Fig.2-1: Flowchart of AMALIA Restaurant System

Step 1: Staff authorize the table for customers to make order

- Staff needs to login for the customers and first screen pops up with
  the button ‘Dine in” or “stake away”, if “take away” is selected then
  it navigates customers to the ordering screen, but if dine is selected
  customers are navigated to Number of customer Screen after they put
  the number of customers then go to second step.

Step 2: Customer views the menu

- Customer able to see categories of food separated by Tabs. Eg. ‘Main
  Dishes’, ‘Noodles’, ‘Drinks’, ‘Appetizers’. Within each tab, price and
  functions which customer can increase/decrease the number of order for
  a menu they want.

Step 3: Customer make order

- All the food menu that customers have selected will be shown in the
  sidebar table and the total number of menu selected. After customer is
  satisfied with the menu, they can proceed to order by tabbing on the
  ‘Order’ button.

Step 4: Organize the order list for chef to see

- Chef will be shown with a list of orders made directly from customers.
  Eg. Order1, Order2. OrderDate: xx/xx/xxxx. Chef can click on an order
  to see its details.

Step 5: Chef accepts orders

- Choose to cook it by clicking ‘Confirm’. The orders will be listed
  accordingly with the time customers made the orders. First come, first
  serve.

Step 6: Chef mark order as ‘Finished’ when it’s done

- Chef will need to update the order status accordingly. Eg. Order1:
  ‘Cooking’, Order2: ‘Finished’, Order3: ‘Ready to serve’.

Step 7: Food Status: ‘Cooking’, ‘Finished’, ‘Served’ for customer
tables.

- From time to time, customer can check their order/food status on their
  table screen.

Step 8: Show receipt on screen and/or print receipt for customer

- Customers are given with two options to check their receipt. They can
  either check it on their table screen and also will be given a printed
  receipt from the staff.

CHAPTER 3

DEVELOPMENT PLATFORM

The development platform of the system will be on web-based. Tools that
we use for developing this system is Visual Studio Microsoft Azure for
front end as Microsoft Azure (formerly Windows Azure) is a cloud
computing service created by Microsoft for building, testing, deploying,
and managing applications and services through a global network of
Microsoft-managed data centers. It provides software as a service
(SaaS), platform as a service and infrastructure as a service and
supports many different programming languages, tools and frameworks,
including both Microsoft-specific and third-party software and systems.
For the Back end we use Microsoft SQL and C# as the programming
language. From Microsoft Azure, we use App Service and SQL Database.

The menu management system and architecture has been designed based on
the general requirements, user survey, functional requirements and
non‐functional requirements elicited from the restaurant.
