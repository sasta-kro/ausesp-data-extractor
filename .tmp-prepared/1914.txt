Abstract

This project focuses on ordering food online from the ABAC cafeteria. It
is a web-based system that is fully responsive. The shop owners can add
the food items in the menu and the customers (students, faculty members,
staffs) can easily place an order simply through the website. Customers
can top-up balance in their e-wallet provided by the system and use it
to order food anytime. This system can be very useful mainly for the
customers and shop owners. It eliminates all the hassles of having to
use coupons. Furthermore, it also reduces the problem of having the go
to the canteen every time to see the food menu by providing the service
of viewing food menu beforehand in the website itself. Finally, this
system is anticipated to be useful for everybody in Assumption
University.

Introduction

Food ordering system (AU Canteen) is mainly useful to the students
because, some of them have a very short lunch break or not at all. So,
with this system, students can easily order the food online while they
are in class and just go pick up the ready food once the class is done
which can save a lot of their crucial time. Based on my investigation
during the project, most of the students have a problem of having to go
to each and every food stall to see what food items are available and
know their respective price so that they can buy the coupons
accordingly. Also, it was found that some of the shop owners lose their
coupons which is detrimental for them. Therefore, there was an idea to
design and develop a website for ordering food online, which address the
problems and/or issues encountered in the current system as well as
provide additional useful functions to fulfil users’ needs. The
stakeholders involved in this system are: customers (students, faculty
members, staffs), shop owner and cashier staff, who will use this
system. Major goals of the system includes providing simple and easy
functions and useful features in order to reduce the problems of all
user roles/stakeholders.

2. Statement of the Problems:

As per the initial survey conducted, I found out the problems existing
in the current system or the problems faced by the students and the shop
owners. As a result of the survey, following are the list of problems
that I found out:

1)  The canteen is usually crowded

We can clearly see from figure 5, the canteen is usually very congested.
The lunch break for most of the students is from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM.
The canteen is spacious but it is not enough when students to go all at
once and wait for the food. When a lot of students go to the canteen to
eat at the same time, the canteen tends to get very crowded which is
troublesome for the students. This problem can again arise another
problem of students being late to the afternoon class due to long queue
in the canteen.

2)  The coupon is not reusable again the next day

Currently, the students who have their meal in the canteen, first have
to buy the coupons at the counter. Once they are done with their meal
and if they still have some coupons remaining, then they have to return
it to the counter right on the same day and get their cash back. If, by
any chance, if they forget to return the coupon, there is no way that
they can use that coupon some other day and that coupon goes to waste.

3)  Confusion about the order

Once the food is ready, the shop owner simply calls out the name of the
food and the students have to go pick it up. If there are multiple
students who ordered the same dish then there can be a confusion and
misunderstanding. The one who had not been waiting for long time might
get the dish before the one who had been waiting for longer. There is no
standard way of giving away the food items to the students once it is
prepared. The system for giving out the order is very random which can
create confusions.

4)  Difficulty in knowing what is available in which stall

The process of knowing what food is available in which stall is very
troublesome currently. According to figure 9, 73.1% of the students (out
of 26 surveyed in total) find out the food menu by visiting each and
every stall and remaining 26.9% find out from their friends who have
already visited the stall on that particular day. When buying the
coupon, they do not know what the prices of the respective food items
are and students can end up buying more or less coupons which can again
lead to problem 2.

5)  Chances of losing coupon

According to figures 10 and 21, we can see there are some students and
shop owners as well who have lost their coupons or misplaced it
somewhere which is not very favorable for both the students and the shop
owners.

6)  Hard to handle coupons

We can clearly see from figures 11 and 22 that 69.2% of the students and
75% of the shop owners are having a difficult time in handling the
coupons.

3. Users’ Functional Requirements

  The web application “Au Canteen” will be very beneficial to all the
  users involved. So, I have outlines all the requirements that will
  satisfy the users’ needs.

  The main users involved in this system are as follows:

A.  Customers (students/faculty members/staffs)

B.  Shop Owner

C.  Cashier Staff

D.  Administrator

Although there are 4 users involved in the system, the main focus will
on the first three users. Everyone who would like to access the system
will have to log-in first.

Customers’ Functional Requirements

1)  View the food menu of all stalls

  The customers will be able to view the food menu of all the food
  stalls available in the cafeteria. They will be able to filter the
  list of food by shop name, type of food (Veg/Non-veg) and name of
  food, or view the whole food menu all at once as per their
  convenience.

2)  Add to cart

  Customers will be able to pick their desired food from various stall
  and add it into the cart.

3)  Checkout and make payment

  The customers will be able to checkout from their cart and make
  payment through the application’s e-wallet system.

4)  Top-up balance

  Customers will be able to top-up their balance easily at the cashier
  counter at any time when the cafeteria is open.

5)  Track status of the food

  Customers will be able to track the status of the food (whether it is
  being prepared or finished) once they place an order.

Shop Owners’ Functional Requirements:

1)  View orders

  The shop owners will be able to easily view the orders placed by the
  customers

2)  Update status of food

  Shop owners will be able to change the food status depending on the
  availability of the food.

3)  Modify Menu

  Shop owners will be able to add new food items, delete or update the
  existing ones from the menu.

4)  View total earned

  Shop owners will be able to view how much they earned on that
  particular day.

Cashier Staff’s Functional Requirements:

1)  Add balance

  Cashier Staff will be able to easily add/top-up balance to customer’s
  account.

2)  Withdraw remaining balance

  Cashier staff will also be able to withdraw remaining balance of the
  customers who want to get their cash back.

Administrator’s Functional Requirements:

1)  Register accounts for Shop Owners and Cashier Staffs

  The admin will be able to register accounts for the shop owners who
  want to open a stall in the canteen and can also register account for
  the cashier staffs.

2)  View all accounts

  Admin will also be able to view all the existing accounts (customer,
  shop owner and cashier staff).

4. Objectives:

  The main objective of this project is to design and develop a food
  ordering system called “AU Canteen” for the easy use. It is a
  responsive web-based application. Following are the objectives of this
  system:

1)  To replace the coupon system

  By replacing the coupon system, a lot of existing problems (like
  losing coupons, misplacing coupons, not being able to use the coupon
  in the next day etc.) can be solved. Instead of using coupons, the new
  application will use e-wallet system where the customers can top-up
  balance and use it any time.

2)  To avoid confusion about the orders

  Another main objective of the new system is to avoid the confusion
  about the orders. With the new system, there will be unique order
  number assigned to the customers each time they order which can be
  shown to the shop owner to pick up the food once it is ready which can
  avoid any kind of confusions.

3)  To provide an easier way to view the food menu of each stall

  With this system, the customers can easily view the food menu on the
  application itself without having to go to each and every stall to
  check.

4)  To save students’ valuable time

  Students will be able to order online and track the status of the food
  with this new system. So, they can go to the canteen to pick up the
  food once it is prepared which can save a lot of their time.

5. Scope of the Project:

  There are four stakeholders involved in this system:

1)  Customer (Students/faculty members/staffs)

2)  Shop Owner

3)  Cashier Staff

4)  Administrator

All users have to perform the following functions in order to be able to
access the system.

Log-in/Log-out

Everyone has to log-in into the system in order to be able to use the
system and access the functions that are provided or log-out if they
want to exit from the system.

Customer:

1)  Make Order

Customers can make an order just by selecting the food from food menu if
they are available. The customers can see the status of the food whether
it is available or not and order accordingly.

2)  View details of order

After customers make an order, the complete order will be shown in the
cart.

3)  Confirm or modify order

In case the customers would like to make some changes after they add the
food to cart, it is possible to do that. They can just tap the cancel
button to delete that particular food item in the cart. Once they are
sure about the order, they can finalize the order by confirming.

4)  Make payment

Once the customers finalize the order, they can check out from the cart
and make the payment for the order.

5)  View order status

After placing the order, customers can view the status of the order
whether it is preparing or finished.

Shop Owner:

1)  Add/Modify menu

Shop owners can add as many food items as they want in the menu. They
can also make changes in the prices of the food items present in the
menu.

2)  View Order

Once the order has been placed by the customers, shop owners can view
the order details.

3)  Change status of order

The shop owners can change the status of the order based on whether the
order is being prepared or finished or picked up.

Cashier Staff:

1)  Top-up balance

The cashier staff can add balance to the customer’s account.

2)  Withdraw remaining balance

If the customers no longer want their money deposited in the e-wallet of
the system, they can go to the cashier staff to withdraw their remaining
balance.

Administrator:

1)  Open account for shop owner and cashier staff

The admin can open an account for shop owners who want to open a shop in
the canteen and for the cashier staffs as well.

6. Limitations:

i.  In order to top-up, they will have to go to the cashier counter.
    (The system does not provide an online banking system to top-up.)

ii. Once the shop owner account is registered, it cannot be again
    modified.

iii. It is a web-based platform which is responsive to mobile devices.

7. Cost and benefit analysis:

I.  Backend Software

  For the backend I decided to use PHP as it is a good option for this
  scale of the project. PHP provides good performance, easy and cheap to
  deploy and maintain and also gives good speed of development.

  For the data storage I have used phpMyAdmin. It is a free and
  open-source administration tool for MySQL. As a portable web
  application written in PHP, it is one on the most popular MySQL
  administration tools, especially for web-hosting services.

  For the domain and web hosting, I have used a web-hosting provider
  called Hostgator which provides affordable domain registration and
  shared servers for web hosting.

  Overall, the total cost for developing this project is TBH 120 which
  is the cost of domain and web hosting together.

III. Benefit Analysis

  AU Canteen food ordering system replaces the disparate systems with a
  single, easy-to use solution that consolidates or merges all
  activities related to placing and viewing orders. By replacing the
  coupon system, it will solve a lot of problems that the students and
  the shop owners are facing with the current system. With this system,
  customers will be able to conveniently view the food menu, place the
  orders and it also enables the shop owners to perform their tasks with
  greater ease.

8. System Analysis and Design:

1)  Project Identification and Selection

  Going to each and every stall to see the food menu, buying the coupon
  according to the price of the food, placing order and waiting for the
  order to get ready in a queue is a very troublesome process since it
  is being done manually. So, my goal in this project is to create a
  same process to view food menu, place order, check order, and make
  payment based on an online website which is responsive which will be
  easier and more flexible to use.

2)  Project Initiation and Planning

  To begin this project, I first of all gathered all the important
  information relating to the user requirements of this system and
  prepared the scope and objectives. I decided to use the Gantt chart
  for the system development, project management and analyze the cost
  and benefit. The result from this phase are scope and limitations,
  objectives, cost and benefits, and user requirement features of the
  proposed system.

3)  Analyzing System needs

  I have analyzed the current situation of the processes in the
  cafeteria and identified the problems that are faced. According to
  that, I have designed a Data Flow Diagram (DFD) and Entity Relation
  Diagram (ERD) for the proposed system.

4)  Designing the proposed system

  Based on the analysis phase, I converted the E-R Diagram into
  relational database model and created DFD for it. User interface are
  designed in this process.

5)  Development of the proposed system

  In this phase, the design on the proposed system is converted into
  computer software, which includes computer programming and translating
  the design specifications into the computer code.

6)  Testing the proposed system

  This phase is about testing whether the programming code and all
  technical parts will work correctly with the conditions in the system
  or not. In this step, the system is tested and also a satisfaction
  survey was conducted for the users to check whether they are satisfied
  with the system developed in this step.

Conclusion:

AU Canteen Food Ordering System will be beneficial to not only help
standardize the current way of system, but also reducing and solving a
lot of problems that we are facing right now, especially customers and
shop owners. With this new system, there will no longer be a confusion
about the orders and the customers will be able to easily view the food
menu of each stall. Also, this system reduce all the problems of coupons
being faced currently. Therefore, this system will be beneficial to use
for all users.
