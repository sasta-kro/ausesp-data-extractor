Abstract

Document flow for Human Resources is an automatic flow with Microsoft
Office software to help the HR and authorised people to handle staff’s
leave requests in ABAC online.

This project aims to eliminate the paperwork for the leave requests,
make it possible to submit the leave requests at anywhere, anytime with
the network. With this project, AU’s staff will no longer need to come
to the university to take the paper form and submit it manually and the
HR personnel will not need to record each request’s information
manually. All the request information will be saved automatically. The
HR personnel can also use MS Power BI to access the data and make it
visualization. It is easy to view the summary of each specific staff in
a specific date interval.

Chapter 1: Introduction

Human Resources jobs include monitoring the work hours of the
organization’s staff. Staff has the need to leave their jobs for several
days and they must fill in the leave request forms. In the past, when
dealing with leave request form, HR faces the problem of unclear
writing, and 3D (document, delivery, and delay). Also, paper documents
are likely to be lost and damaged. This newly developed system allows HR
to make decision online to save time and money and ensure the safety of
the documents.

[]

1.1 Problem Statement

This project aims to build a system that will improve the following
issues.

1.  Time: With the present system using paper form, the requesters have
    to come to the university to get the form and fill it. Then, the
    request form will be passed to three or four authorized persons,
    manually.

2.  Cost: With the present system, the university needs to print a form
    for each request. The university also needs to employ some staff at
    the office to keep the forms in file. The university will pay for
    the cost of paper, print fee, equipment maintenance and salary. The
    cost is estimated at 13,100 baht each month. (Salary may come to
    10,000 baht per month, papers for 1 ream of 500 pieces at 100 baht
    per ream, toner at 1,000 baht, and monthly equipment maintenance at
    2,000 baht.)

3.  Safety: Recently, with the pandemic of COVID-19, people are afraid
    of making contact with others. For safety reasons, it is better to
    stay at home. In this situation, the staff may not want to come to
    school to contact with others who will fill the leave form.

1.2 Objective

1.  Build an efficient document flow process: In each leave request, it
    must be approved by four authorized persons of the department. In
    the old system, the paper form must be manually passed to all levels
    upwards. The process will eventually be transferred to HR team to
    manage the documents.

2.  The system allows HR staff to view the history of record and
    summarize in MS Power BI.

3.  Generate a printable form for the Leave Request.

1.3 Scope of the Project

1.  This project is built on Microsoft Power Automate (MPA) and
    Microsoft Teams (MT) platform. AU has an enterprise licence of
    Microsoft teams and this is an enhancement of what they already
    have. Receive the information from MS Form and make the Approval
    Process automatically transfer to different Authorized Persons or
    the Leave Requester with MS Power Automate.

2.  Make each kind of Leave Request accessible and filled with the MS
    Form by the Leave Requesters.

Chapter 2: Related Work

STEVE GOODMAN [1] posted his idea of automating the process of creating
a sales or project proposal. The solution is to provide a form to users
to provide information, allow a manager to review the information
entered and then automatically create a Microsoft Word document based on
a company template. They created an underlying Team Contoso Proposals to
provision a SharePoint site and to use as the place to access the form
and for approval management. They also created a PowerApp form that
presents a friendly, easy to use interface to their users, then
integrated it with Microsoft Teams. They then created the Microsoft Flow
that glues the process together and set up the approvals process within
the Flow.

LUISE FREESE [2] uses Microsoft Flow to automate her process of sending
stickers. She created a form in Microsoft Forms for users to fill in and
it can automatically form a Word Document and a SharePoint list.
Therefore, every time a new form is submitted, a new Word Document,
which can be an envelope or label is generated and once a day she will
get a summary of all submissions in her SharePoint list.

ANDREW WARLAND [3] presented his idea of auto-populating document
templates via a form in SharePoint. The benefits include auto-creates
one or more Word documents (multiple based on metadata choices contained
in the form), auto-populates the Word documents where required with the
metadata in the form stores that document (or documents) in a folder
(actually a document set) that can be used to add other content. The
processes are setting up the model, setting up the document library,
linking the metadata columns with the template, summary, and outcomes of
the first stage and creating the workflow.

The group learned from the above examples to design forms for requesters
to fill in, automatically receive forms and give access to different
authorised persons to reply to the requesters. As well the group formed
an Excel files of request history for HR to view and check.

Chapter 3: Proposed Methodology

3.1 Technology Overview

Since the system is based on Microsoft Teams and Power Automate
platform, the following list is the overview of the technology used for
this project.

1.  MS Forms: Collect information form the requesters.

2.  MS Power Automate: Make the approval process automatically.

3.  MS Teams: Send the alert to remind the user to check and make the
    decisions for each request and show the result of the request.

4.  MS OneDrive: Save the printable form.

5.  MS Power BI: Data visualization, dashboard.

6.  PHP: Ask the backend server to interact with the MySQL database and
    Microsoft Power Automate.

7.  MySQL: Use to store staffs’ information and Leave records.

4.2 System Design (consider items that applied)

1.  Process / Workflow / Sequence Diagram

  []

Figure 4-3: Workflow

  Figure 4-3 is the process of the system. Once a requester fills any
  kind of leave form, it will trigger the flow. The flow will send the
  approval to each authorized person one by one. In the approval
  process, if anyone among the authorized persons reject the request,
  the flow will stop and send the result to the requester. The request
  will be successful when all the authorized persons approve the
  request.

2.  System Architecture

[]

  Figure 4-4 is the system architecture. Once a requester fills the form
  on Microsoft forms, it will trigger the flow. The power automate will
  use the requester’s name and send a HTTP request to the PHP server.
  The PHP server uses the name to query the related data from MySQL
  database and return the data. If the requester’s data is inside the
  database, the flow will send the form’s information to the authorized
  person and PHP server. The PHP server will insert the new record to
  MySQL database. When the authorized person makes decision for this
  request, it will interact with power automate and trigger the new
  action. It will send the request to the next authorized person or send
  to the result to request. It depends on the previous authorized
  person’s decision. At the same time, the power automate will send HTTP
  request to PHP server to update the related record.

Chapter 6: Conclusion

The group made this system by using Microsoft software to digitalize the
Leave Request and eliminate the costly paperwork. With the online
request, the whole process becomes easier and faster. The group think
this solution is feasible and it is already tested by the HR people.
With their feedback, the group removed some questions from the forms and
made it more concise. When the requester fills the form, they just need
to answer a few questions. The other information such as authorized
leave days, sick leave days, and annual leave days already taken, will
be retrieved and calculated from backend server. As shown in the
demonstration to the HR personnel, the latest version works well.

The group found some limitations. If the requester’s information was
retrieved from database, all the staff’s information must be recorded
into the database in advance. So when a staff whose information is not
in the database fills a form, the request cannot be sent to the
authorised persons. In the current version, all the departments have
four authorized persons. Different departments have different number of
authorized persons. The group think the Power BI’s visualization summary
is not enough.

In the future, the group will record all the university’s staff’s
information into the database and consult all the departments the number
of their authorized persons. This system can handle all the departments’
requests based on each department's rules. The group will make more and
various meaningful Power BI’s visualization summaries.

This project is a good experience for the group to learn about making
Microsoft Power Automate flow. With the experience, the group learn how
to work together to meet the requirements of users.
